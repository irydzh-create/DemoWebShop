Homework 01 – Observations

Website: https://demowebshop.tricentis.com/

I compared 10 HTML tags using Selenium, SelectorsHub and Chrome DevTools Console.

7 out of 10 tags returned identical results across all three tools.

The following differences were observed:

Tag   | Selenium | SelectorsHub | Console
div   | 182      | 165          | 182
img   | 42       | 25           | 25
span  | 23       | 24           | 23

Additional investigation:

Without waiting, Selenium found 10 img and 150 div elements.

With time.sleep(5), different runs returned:
- 25 img and 165 div elements.
- 42 img and 182 div elements.

Possible reasons:
- Dynamic DOM changes.
- Asynchronous loading.
- Different execution times.
- Browser extensions.

Conclusion:

Both TAG_NAME and CSS_SELECTOR returned identical results for all 10 tags in Selenium.

A fixed waiting time does not guarantee stable results. In future tests, WebDriverWait should be used to wait for specific conditions.
